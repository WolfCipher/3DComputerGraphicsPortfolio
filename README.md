# Portfolio 2 for Honors CS559, Spring 2025

View the project here: https://wolfcipher.github.io/3DComputerGraphicsPortfolio/

There are 8 pages on this portfolio:
1) Snowman
2) Articulated Quadcopters and Radars
3) Amusement Park Rides
4) Construction Equipment
5) Buildings
6) Vehicle (with Reflection)
7) Real Object
8) Student's Choice

## Snowman
There is a snowman and a snowdog with animated articulated motion. The carrot nose/ears are textured with a bump map.
## Articulated Quadcopters and Radars
My quadcopters, which are designed to look like a bee and a fly, move along curved paths and are articulated with propellers. My radars, which look like flowers, track the quadcopters' movements. The bee's stripes are made with color mapping.
## Amusement Park Rides
I made use of rotations to create the flying swing ride, carousel, and tire swing. The tire is textured with a bump map, and the tire swing's wood frame is textured with a color map.
## Construction Equipment
The bodies of the equipment were made with extruded curves. Additionally, the user can translate and rotate the equipment. The user can also change the state of the equipment.
## Buildings
Buildings and plants were designed with BufferGeometry and other geometry. They were textured with texture mapping (color, bump, normal, and environment maps). There is a reflective object in the scene. The user can also press (b) to switch between the regular OrbitControls camera and the bird's eye view camera that follows a path.
## Vehicle (with Reflection)
I made a moving wagon, with articulated wheels. There are two reflective objects. The user can press (c) to switch between the regular OrbitControls camera and the camera that follows the wagon's path.
## Real Object
I chose to create a book. I mapped pictures of a real book that I took onto a geometry. I edited the roughness and metalness of the geometry so that the cover was glossy and the pages were not. 
## Student's Choice
I recreated my favorite childhood toy. I recreated the toy's textures by mapping pictures that I took of the toy and by editing roughness as needed. The toy is articulated and animated.
