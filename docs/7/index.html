Page 7: Real Object

Make a Real Object by Texturing a Simple Object

I took pictures of the front, back, spine, and pages of the book Skandar and the Phantom Rider, which I then put together into a single image in Paint 3D. This image is the texture of the book.

I also modified the roughness and metalness of the book (at the cover, not at the pages) so that it shines like the book I took the pictures of. (The book has a glossy cover made of plastic.) You can best see this when you look at the book from the top.

The carpet is also a texture I made by taking a picture. This picture was not modified in any app, but I added a blue tinge when creating the material.

The files are 09-01-01.js and 09-01-01.html

Requirements

Model some real object using simple geometry and textures. You have to make the textures (for example, by taking a photograph). The example I showed in the workbook was the textbook.

The Workbook 9 Page 1 Instructions

Create some real-world object with a texture. Look around and see if there’s something you want to model: your laptop, a book, etc. This is a good opportunity to incorporate real photos in a texture.

A goal here is to use simple shapes, but more complex textures, to make an object that is interesting. And the important thing is that you make it (so, you need to at least take the photograph yourself). You need to make something other than the Foundamentals of Computer Graphics book.
