Student’s Choice

Here’s an empty world. Make something.

Meet my favorite childhood toy, Colby Chester.

All of the knit textures you see were made by taking pictures of the toy from many sides and editing the pictures together with Paint 3D. I also took a picture to create the carpet texture (the color map and bump map for the floor), but I did not modify it in any app.

Notice that the center of the eyes appear to be made from smooth plastic, resembling the hard beads on the real toy, while the rest of the dog is made of rougher knit textures.

Also notice that the toy's head rotates up and down, and the toy's tail swishes from side to side.

The files are page9.js and page9.html
